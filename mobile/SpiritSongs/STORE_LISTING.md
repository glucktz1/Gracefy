## Full Description (4000 characters max)

### English Version:

Gracefy is your ultimate destination for Christian music streaming. Discover, stream, and download thousands of worship songs, gospel music, and hymns - all in one beautifully designed app.

**Features:**

🎵 **Extensive Music Library**
Browse thousands of Christian songs from talented artists and choirs across Tanzania and East Africa. From traditional hymns to contemporary worship, find music that speaks to your soul.

🔊 **High-Quality Streaming**
Enjoy crystal-clear audio streaming optimized for all network conditions. Our smart player adapts to your connection for uninterrupted worship.

📥 **Offline Downloads**
Download your favorite songs and playlists to listen offline. Perfect for worship on the go, even without internet access.

📖 **Bible Integration**
Read and listen to Bible verses with text-to-speech audio. Daily devotionals to strengthen your faith journey.

🎧 **Personalized Experience**
- Create custom playlists
- Like and save your favorite songs
- Get personalized recommendations based on your listening habits
- Continue where you left off across devices

⛪ **Discover Churches & Choirs**
Explore music from local churches and choirs. Find new artists and support your community's worship ministry.

📅 **Neno la Leo (Daily Word)**
Start each day with inspiring scripture readings and reflections from religious leaders.

🌙 **Beautiful Design**
Elegant dark theme designed for comfortable viewing. Simple navigation to find exactly what you need.

**Categories Include:**
- Sifa na Kuabudu (Praise & Worship)
- Tenzi (Hymns)
- Injili (Gospel)
- Nyimbo za Pasaka (Easter Songs)
- Nyimbo za Kwaresima (Lenten Songs)
- Nyimbo za Krismasi (Christmas Songs)

Download Gracefy today and let music lift your spirit closer to God!
