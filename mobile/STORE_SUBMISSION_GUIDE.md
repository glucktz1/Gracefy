## 📋 Content Rating

### Google Play Store
- **Primary Category:** Music & Audio
- **Content Rating:** Everyone (no objectionable content)
- **Contains Ads:** No (or Yes if you have ads)
- **In-app Purchases:** Yes (for subscriptions)

### Apple App Store
- **Primary Category:** Music
- **Secondary Category:** Lifestyle
- **Age Rating:** 4+ (no objectionable content)
- **In-app Purchases:** Yes

## 🚀 Next Steps

1. **Download the generated images** from the URLs above
2. **Take app screenshots** on a real device or emulator
3. **Create the privacy policy page** on gracefy.net
4. **Upload to Google Play Console:**
   - Go to https://play.google.com/console
   - Create new app → Fill details → Upload APK
5. **Upload to App Store Connect:**
   - Requires iOS build (needs Mac)
   - Go to https://appstoreconnect.apple.com
   - Create new app → Fill details → Upload IPA
